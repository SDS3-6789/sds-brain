# Investigation

## Entry conditions and scope

Customer, cluster, backend identity, installed build, symptom, time window and approved read access.

## Bounded evidence collection

Explain each read-only check, expected output, cost and sensitive fields. Label commands as proposed until actually executed.

## Decision points

For each hypothesis, state the evidence supporting or weakening it. Do not jump from correlation to root cause.

## Escalation and exit criteria

Record when to stop, what to send to the owner/vendor and how to verify the investigation is complete. Keep proposed changes separate from read-only diagnostics.

## Sources and review

Exact versioned references, owner/reviewer and last environment validation.
