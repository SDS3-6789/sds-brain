# Incident record

## Impact and scope

Affected customer/cluster/service, start/end UTC, severity, status and user-visible impact. Store this under the matching restricted private context.

## Timeline

UTC timestamp, observation/action, actor, evidence reference and result. Mark suggested actions that were not executed.

## Evidence and hypotheses

Redacted request IDs, relevant health/metrics/log references, confidence and alternative explanations. Never include secret keys or raw authorization data.

## Resolution and verification

Actual approved changes, validation results, remaining risks and rollback evidence where relevant.

## Learning

What can become an anonymized shared runbook, what stays customer-private, owner and review date.
